What the badges mean

Lean checked means Lean accepted an unconditional kernel theorem (.thmInfo) for the formal statement shown in the Lean view, using only Lean’s standard axioms. Lean checked (proof supplied outside manuscript) means Lean checked a proof for a manuscript remark, proposition, or skipped step stated without proof in the PDF; the supplied argument is collected in supplement.md. Step-verified (paper) means an opt-in 1:1 step_map passed compiled-Expr auditUses verification and every step’s origin: “paper” excerpt is a verbatim substring of the bound TeX; Step-verified (with supplied steps) means the same 1:1 check passed with Lean-supplied intermediate steps collected in supplement.md. Conditional means Lean checked a sorry-free proof assuming one or more unproved prerequisite statements. Subproofs checked means parts are checked while obligations remain open. Needs author confirmation flags an unattended triage reading awaiting review. Machine verified on GitHub means a separate GitHub run repeated that check at the published commit. Multi-kernel / Comparator means opt-in paranoid GitHub CI additionally checked the hash-locked Challenge.lean/Solution.lean harness offline through independent kernel checker(s) (Comparator, lean4checker, or Lean ≥ v4.35.0 lake check --paranoid). Author reviewed means a named person compared that formal statement with the paper and accepted it.

Neither Lean badge says the formal statement is the paper’s theorem. To judge that, read the Lean view beside the Paper view. The Plain English and Lamport proof views are explanations, not machine-checked, and Lean’s proof may take a different route from the paper’s. Standard checks catch mistakes, not proof files written to fool them; for proofs you do not trust, enable paranoid CI with a dedicated checker such as Comparator or lean4checker.
